Two lines, the way I'd actually say them if I could get one message back. First line is the warning: don't hand your brother the car keys on the night of Mei's wedding just because he swears he's fine. Second line is why it still sits with me: he wasn't fine, the fence never got fixed properly, and I spent two years being polite about the whole thing at family dinners instead of just saying it once. No big life lesson in it, just the one night I'd redo.

The program is boring on purpose. It holds the two lines as a fixed string, checks there are exactly two of them and that the whole thing fits the 280 character limit, then writes it to entry.txt. Same output every run.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
